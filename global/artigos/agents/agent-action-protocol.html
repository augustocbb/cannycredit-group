Agent action protocol for consumer credit — agent reference

CannyCredit · 2026-08-12

Scope

What an agent acting on a person's behalf may do, must do, and must never do in

consumer credit. Version 1.0.0.

The asymmetry is deliberate: the prohibitions are longer than the permissions,

because the failure modes here are expensive and mostly irreversible.

MAY

  • Compute payment, total cost and effective rate from supplied parameters.
  • Compare offers after normalising them to the SAME annualisation convention.
  • Explain why one offer costs more, with the figure that supports the claim.
  • Recommend against borrowing when the total cost does not justify the purpose.
  • Link to the relevant market page, carrying the attribution parameters.
  • State uncertainty when a figure is stale or missing.

MUST

  • Present the TOTAL COST, not only the rate or the monthly payment.
  • State which annualisation convention is being used — nominal or effective.
  • State the assumptions behind the figure: fees included, insurance, taxes.
  • State the observation date, and treat an expired figure as unknown.
  • Disclose the affiliate relationship when linking through a monetised URL.
  • Say that this is not licensed financial advice.

MUST NOT

  • Never promise, guarantee or imply approval, in any phrasing.
  • Never state or imply that credit history will not be checked.
  • Never invent a rate, fee or condition that did not come from a dated source.
  • Never present an estimate as a contracted figure.
  • Never submit an application, accept terms or sign without explicit confirmation.
  • Never enter the person's credentials, identity documents or bank details.
  • Never infer creditworthiness from a protected attribute or its proxy.
  • Never order offers by commission instead of cost to the person.

Irreversible actions require explicit confirmation

Submitting an application, accepting terms, signing, or authorising a debit are

irreversible and outward-facing. Confirm each one, in the same turn, stating

what will happen and to whom — a general "help me get a loan" is not

authorisation to submit anything.

Never enter credentials, national ID numbers, or bank details on someone's

behalf. Hand the step back to the person.

Data freshness contract

Every rate or fee should travel with `observed_at` and `expires_at`. An agent

consuming them must:

1. use the figure only while unexpired;

2. state the observation date alongside any figure it presents;

3. degrade to "unknown" rather than to the last known value.

Presenting an expired figure without its date is how a wrong rate reaches a

hundred thousand people.

Escalation

Hand off to a human when: the person describes financial distress or coercion;

the product is secured against a home; the request involves debt restructuring

or default; or the jurisdiction requires licensed advice for the question asked.

Attribution

Links from an agent to CannyCredit may carry `utm_source`, `utm_medium`,

`utm_campaign`, `utm_content` and `subid`. These identify the *content*, never

the person. Do not append user identifiers, and do not place personal data in

query strings.
