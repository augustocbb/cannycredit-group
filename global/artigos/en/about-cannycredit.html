About CannyCredit

CannyCredit · 2026-08-12

How we started

CannyCredit started from a simple, uncomfortable observation: almost

everyone compares loans by the monthly payment, and almost no lender makes

it easy to compare them by total cost. The monthly payment is the number

the advert prints large. It is also the easiest number to move — just

stretch the term.

The result is a market where the offer that looks cheapest is often the

most expensive. Not through fraud, but through asymmetry of technical

knowledge. On one side of the table sits someone who calculates interest

for a living. On the other, someone deciding under financial pressure.

CannyCredit exists to narrow that gap. We gather the offers available in

each market, apply the same assumptions to all of them, and show the

figure that actually supports a decision. We publish across 53 markets,

each in its own language and its own terminology — APR, CAT, CET and RRSO

are not the same thing and do not translate by approximation.

Our objective

That anyone, in any market where we operate, can answer three questions

before signing: what will I pay in total, what alternatives exist, and

what changes if I wait.

Mission

To secure for everyone the widest and best possible access to credit, backed by the technical knowledge needed to understand what is being signed.

Vision

A market where nobody accepts a loan without knowing its total cost.

Values

Clarity before conversion

When the right answer is "do not take this loan", that is what we publish. A comparison that can only say yes is not a comparison.

Figures with provenance

Every rate we publish carries a source and a date. We do not estimate what we can verify, and we flag what we have not yet confirmed.

Declared independence

We earn through affiliate partnerships and we say so on every page. Offers are ordered by cost to you, not by commission to us.

Access for everyone

Credit explained well cannot be a privilege of those who already understand finance, or of those who speak English.

CannyCredit is not a financial institution and does not lend money. We compare third-party offers and may earn a commission when you take out a product through our links. This does not change the ordering or the calculations shown.
